LUMEN presence check

Your screen will show a soft, changing colour light for about 100 seconds. Your camera is sent to the verifier, who measures how your face responds to that light. Nothing is installed.

  1. Screen brightness to 100%, charger plugged in.
  2. Sit about 40 cm from the screen, facing it, and keep still while it runs.
  3. Dim or steady room light is best; avoid a lamp shining on your face.

Allow the camera when the browser asks.

Esc stops. The light is designed to be comfortable, but stop if you are sensitive to flicker.
